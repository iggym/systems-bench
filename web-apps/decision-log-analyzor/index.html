Decision Log Analyzer

Analyzes an agent's decision log for reasoning transparency: which decisions repeat, how well self-reported confidence predicts outcomes (calibration), where failures cluster, and which decisions cost the most. Consumes the decisionLog schema v1.

Schema: decisions[] with step, actor, decision, optionsConsidered, rationale, confidence (0–1), outcome (success|failure|partial), latencyMs, costUsd, error. Confidence is self-reported — calibration here measures how well that self-report tracks reality.

Calibration buckets are 0–0.5, 0.5–0.7, 0.7–0.9, 0.9–1.0. A bucket with high confidence but >20% failure is flagged as a calibration risk — the dangerous case where the agent believes it's right and isn't. Thresholds are heuristics; treat as hypotheses, then read the cases.

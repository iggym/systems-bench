Meter Shock

Sees the blowout before the bill does: projects your usage-based spend to month-end from today's burn rate, and tells you the exact day you cross your budget.

Projection READY
Math: burn/day = spend ÷ elapsed days; projected month-end = burn/day × days in cycle; blowout day = budget ÷ burn/day. The 7-day window is compared to the month-to-date window to catch acceleration or deceleration.

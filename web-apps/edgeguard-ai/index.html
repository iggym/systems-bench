EdgeGuard AI — dual-engine hedge router

Primary Groq call at t=0. If it has not returned by the hedge threshold, a Gemini Flash hedge is fired at that moment. First to complete wins; the in-flight loser is aborted via AbortController. If the primary wins before the threshold, the hedge never fires and no second call is billed.

Cost is an estimate: input tokens counted from the prompt; output billed at max tokens as a worst case. Real billing follows actual tokens.
